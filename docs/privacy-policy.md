# Internably Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 5, 2026  
**Last updated:** November 5, 2026

## 1. Scope

Internably, LLC (“Internably,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) operates the Internably website, the Internably iOS application, and related services (collectively, the “Services”). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, how long we retain it, and the choices available to you.

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Website application and verification information

When you apply to join Internably through our website, we may collect:

- First and last name
- School or university
- School email address
- Internship company, city, state, and work email address
- Internship offer letter, acceptance screenshot, proof of enrollment, or similar eligibility documentation
- Industry interests
- LinkedIn profile URL, if provided
- Demographic information such as gender and ethnicity when requested through the application form

We use demographic information to understand and improve community participation. We do not use it to make unlawful discriminatory decisions.

### B. Account and profile information

When you create or use an Internably account, we may collect:

- Email address and username
- Password, which is stored in hashed form rather than plain text
- Name, pronouns, headline, biography, city, school, major, degree, graduation year, internship company, skills, interests, and professional links
- Profile photo and cover photo
- Networking and internship-availability preferences
- Account verification, approval status, and role

### C. Content, connections, and communications

We collect information you create or submit through the Services, including:

- Posts, images, comments, and likes
- Group memberships and activity
- Connection requests and accepted connections
- Private messages and posts shared through messages
- Reports, blocks, and other safety-related actions
- Notifications and read status

Profile information and content you post may be visible to other Internably members depending on the feature and your settings. Private messages are intended for their participants, but may be accessed when reasonably necessary to investigate reports, protect users, maintain security, or comply with law.

### D. Google sign-in information

If you choose Google sign-in, we receive information authorized by Google and you, such as your verified email address, name, and profile picture. We use this information to authenticate you and create or update your Internably account.

### E. Photos, media, and device permissions

When you choose a profile, cover, or post image, the app accesses the image you select from your photo library and uploads it for use in the Services. Internably does not access photos you do not select through this feature.

If push notifications are enabled, we may collect a device push token and platform information needed to deliver notifications.

### F. Website, device, and technical information

We and our service providers may automatically receive limited technical information, such as:

- IP address
- Browser, device, and operating-system information
- App or website request logs, timestamps, and error information
- Cookie and similar-technology data from the website
- General usage and security information

We use this information to operate, secure, diagnose, and improve the Services.

## 3. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

- Review applications and verify eligibility
- Create, authenticate, and administer accounts
- Provide profiles, posts, groups, connections, messaging, sharing, and notifications
- Help members discover relevant peers, groups, opportunities, and events
- Process media uploads
- Provide support and respond to privacy requests
- Detect fraud, abuse, spam, security incidents, and violations of our rules
- Moderate content and investigate reports
- Maintain, analyze, troubleshoot, and improve the Services
- Send account, verification, security, feature, and service communications
- Comply with applicable law and protect the rights, safety, and integrity of Internably and its users

We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not use personal information for third-party targeted advertising.

## 4. How We Share Information

We may share information as follows:

### With other Internably members

Information that you add to your profile, posts, comments, likes, group activity, and connection activity may be visible to other members depending on the feature and your settings. Messages are shared with the participants in the conversation.

### With service providers

We use service providers to operate the Services. These currently include providers for:

- Website hosting and forms, such as Wix
- API hosting and database infrastructure, such as Railway
- Media hosting and delivery, such as Cloudinary
- Authentication, such as Google
- Transactional email and account communications, such as our configured email provider

These providers may process information only to perform services for us or as otherwise permitted by law. We require providers that receive user data to protect it consistently with this Privacy Policy and applicable requirements.

### For legal, safety, and business reasons

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, enforce our policies, investigate misuse, protect users or the public, or protect Internably’s rights and systems. If Internably is involved in a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction subject to appropriate protections.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services, maintain security, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

You can initiate deletion of your Internably account from **Settings → Delete Account** in the iOS app. Account deletion removes the account and associated records from our active application database, except information we are legally required or permitted to retain. Some information may remain temporarily in backups, security logs, or service-provider systems before deletion is completed. We will take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify those copies according to applicable retention schedules.

Content shared with others may remain in their own records or screenshots. Aggregated or de-identified information that cannot reasonably identify you may also be retained.

You may also contact us to request access, correction, or deletion.

## 6. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. These include encrypted transmission where supported, hashed passwords, token protections, role-based access, and restricted administrative access. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing.

Within the app, you can:

- Update profile information
- Control whether you are open to networking
- Block or unblock users
- Manage photo-library permission through iOS Settings
- Log out
- Delete your account

You can also manage Google account access through your Google account settings. To submit a privacy request, contact us using the information below. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

## 8. Age Requirement

Internably is intended for users who are at least 16 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If we learn that an account or application belongs to a person under 16, we will take reasonable steps to delete the information. A parent or guardian who believes a person under 16 has provided information may contact us.

## 9. International Processing

Internably is based in the United States. Information may be processed and stored in the United States and other locations where our service providers operate, subject to applicable protections.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our Services or legal obligations change. We will post the updated policy on this page and revise the “Last updated” date. When appropriate, we may also provide notice through the Services or by email.
